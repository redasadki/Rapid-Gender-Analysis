# Cross-cutting quality criteria and failure modes

## Quality criteria

- Imperfection principle: a good-enough analysis delivered in time beats a perfect one delivered late. State the limits plainly.
- Audience and clarity: match the named reader. No jargon. Define unavoidable technical terms immediately.
- Practicality: every recommendation must be actionable with resources likely to exist.
- Source triangulation: combine quantitative and qualitative evidence and say which is which. Label comparative or historical data. Never rest a recommendation on a single unverified source.
- Structural analysis over cultural description: when citing norms as barriers, trace how the crisis, displacement, or geopolitical history reshaped or weaponised them. Locate harm in structures, never in a population group.
- Political economy: do not assume market participation is inherently liberating. Recognise unpaid care and solidarity as economic contributions. Consider community and non-state justice systems alongside formal law.
- Epistemic stance: no deficit lens. Show local knowledge as primary text. Identify indigenous structures that already perform functions the response might present as new.
- Resource and power transfer: document what money, material support, or decision power moved to local actors. If nothing moved, say so.

## Failure modes

Check the draft against each before finalising.

- Women shown only as a vulnerable group, with the responder as the sole actor.
- Risk traced to roles or customs while war, economy, funding cuts, and collapsed services drop out of the sentence.
- Gender action recommended in the abstract, with no named local women-led organisation to resource or lead.
- "Add women and stir": noting disproportionate impact, then writing recommendations that can be filed and ignored.
- Analysis about local women rather than with or by them.
- Overclaiming from a single figure when the sex split is contested or moving.
- Interpretation buried at the end of a data list.
- Generic label headings.
- Recommendation components embedded in prose.
- Self-check left in the deliverable.
- Provenance and limitations statement placed before the executive summary.
