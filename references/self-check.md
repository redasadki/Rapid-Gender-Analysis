# Self-check

Mandatory quality gate before delivery. Answer each question in one line. If any answer is no, revise. Remove the self-check from the final document.

1. Does the document open with a short institutional statement naming the producer, date, and method basis, followed immediately by the executive summary?
2. Is every quotation the exact wording of its source?
3. Is every statistic traced to a dated primary source, with nothing invented?
4. Does the analysis show affected groups as agents, not only as victims?
5. Is the risk of each group explained through structure, not culture alone?
6. Does every section lead with the analytical conclusion before the supporting evidence?
7. Does each recommendation present actor, action, link to finding, and budget line or decision seat as labelled sub-items?
8. Do the recommendations include unrestricted non-competitive funding to local women-led organisations, a decision-making seat, sex and age disaggregation, and a co-authored follow-up?
9. Is there a Limitations section placed after the recommendations?
10. Does the Limitations section cover data basis, who was not consulted, whose knowledge is credited, non-representative visibility, framework mismatch, and resource transfer?
11. Can the named audience read the executive summary in two minutes and act on it?
12. Does the References section contain every cited source plus background documents, each with full detail and a visible URL?
13. Has the self-check been removed from the final deliverable?
